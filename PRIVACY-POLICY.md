# Privacy Policy — Dahua Configurator

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Dahua Configurator is an independent, local-network camera tool. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Dahua Technology.

## What the app stores on your phone

- Camera connection details you enter: name, IP address or hostname, port,
  username, password, channel, and stream preference.
- App preferences (picture-in-picture, preview, reconnect, clock sync, logging,
  recording limits).
- Optional diagnostic logs, only after you turn on “Record diagnostic logs”.
- Clips you explicitly save to the phone's Movies storage.

All of this is stored on your device. The app has no user accounts, no
analytics, no advertising, and no cloud service. Nothing is sent to the
developer.

## Network access

The app connects directly to cameras on your local network over DVRIP
(TCP 37777) and RTSP (TCP 554), and performs a local discovery broadcast.
Camera credentials are sent only to the camera you configured.

## Microphone

Two-way audio uses your microphone only while you have started it. The audio is
streamed live to the selected camera on your local network. The app does not
record, store, or upload microphone audio. A disclosure is shown before the
microphone is first used, and the feature is never active unless you start it.

## Diagnostic logs

Diagnostic logging is off by default. When enabled, the app writes connection,
playback, recording, and error events to a private file on your phone. Logs
never include your camera password. You can share or clear the log from App
settings; the app itself never uploads it.

## Data sharing and retention

The developer does not collect or share any data. Data stays on your device
until you delete the app, clear its data, or clear the log. Uninstalling the app
removes its private storage.

## Children

The app is a utility for network cameras and is not directed at children.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published with a new app version.
